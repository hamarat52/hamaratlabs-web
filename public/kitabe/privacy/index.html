Kitabe: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Published by: hamaratlabs (Yusuf Hamarat) · Contact: yusuf.hamarat@ozu.edu.tr

Kitabe is a mobile app for reading the historic inscriptions of Istanbul. This page explains what the app does with data.

In short

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not collect, send to a server, sell or share any personal data.

Information that stays on your device

  • Saved items and preferences (the inscriptions and buildings you saved, whether you have seen the welcome/thank-you screen, your language choice, what the map shows) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Location: If you grant permission, your location is used only on the device to show the inscribed buildings nearest to you and to calculate distances. It is never stored or sent anywhere. Without permission the app works without location. You can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Read aloud: The "Listen" feature uses the device's own text-to-speech engine.

Internet connection

  • Inscription texts, photographs, monument information and site sketches are included in the app.
  • To show the map background (OpenStreetMap tiles) your device sends a standard request to the tile server; as with any internet request, your IP address reaches that server. The app adds no personal information to these requests. The tile server's own policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • When you tap the "Osmanlı Kitabeleri Projesi (ottomaninscriptions.com)" link, the website opens in your device's browser; from then on that website's and your browser's policies apply.
  • "Directions" opens the maps app on your device; the app passes it only the name of the building you chose and its coordinates from the source, never your location.
  • The Share button opens your device's share sheet; you choose what to share and with whom.
  • "Report a problem / send a suggestion" opens your device's email app. The draft contains the app version and your device's operating system version; you can see, change or delete them before sending. The email is sent only if you send it.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date renewed.

Content source

Inscription texts and photographs come from the Osmanlı Kitabeleri Projesi (ottomaninscriptions.com). Rights holders can write to the address below about any content; requests are handled promptly.
