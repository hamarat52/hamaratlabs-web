Kitabe: Support

Kitabe is an app for reading the historic inscriptions of Istanbul together with their sources. For questions, bug reports or suggestions, write to us.

Contact

Email: yusuf.hamarat@ozu.edu.tr

You can also write from inside the app: Settings → Report a problem / send a suggestion. The draft includes the app and OS version.

Frequently asked

Do I need an internet connection? Inscription texts and photographs are stored in the app. Only the map background needs internet.

Do I have to allow location? No. If you allow it, the nearest buildings come first on the map. Location stays on your device; it is never stored or sent.

I found a mistake in an inscription. Email us with the record number. Texts are based on the Osmanlı Kitabeleri Projesi (ottomaninscriptions.com); we check corrections against the source.

What is simplified Turkish? Some inscriptions have a version in plain modern Turkish. It is prepared with AI assistance based on cited references and is labelled as such. The source text always comes first.

Do I need an account? No. Kitabe has no accounts; your saved items stay on your device.
