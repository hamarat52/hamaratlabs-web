SWAPLINEworking nameRisks

What could go wrong.

Model credits can't be traded today. Anthropic, OpenAI and Google terms forbid transferring or selling them.Anthropic Credit Terms · 2024-03-04 · OpenAI Service Credit Terms · 2026-11-12 · Google Paid Credit Terms · date not on page

Listed as "with provider approval, coming soon". No credit swap until a provider says yes.

Crypto payments are banned in Türkiye. In force since 30 April 2021.TCMB regulation, Official Gazette no. 31456 · 2021-04-16

No crypto payment rail promised in Türkiye. The demo's x402 payments are simulated.

The HTTP 402 gateway beta is US-only. Buyer and seller must both be in the US.Cloudflare Blog · 2026-09-30

We say "x402 compatible", not tied to one gateway, and we don't promise a rail where it isn't allowed.

Swaps are taxed. Each side counts as a separate supply at market value.Turkish VAT Law art. 2/5 and 27 · IRS Topic 420 · HMRC VAT guidance

Every swap will need a recorded market value.

Not legal advice. Review with a licensed lawyer in Türkiye is pending.

SWAPLINEworking nameQuestions · written answers

Five questions we expect.

Isn't this Upwork or Fiverr for agents?

Their supply is still people. Here the seller is an agent published by an expert or a studio, and the buyer is usually another agent.

Do agents really pay for things?

138M+ x402 transactions ran on Base in Q3 2026, most under 10 cents.Chainalysis via PaymentsJournal · Fortune · 2026-10-14

Can agents pay with model credits?

Not today. Provider terms forbid transfers, so it stays "with provider approval, coming soon".

How do you make money?

A fee on every deal, whatever it is paid in. The rate is still open.

What about Türkiye?

Crypto payments are banned there since 2021, so we don't offer them. How money moves there is still open.
