Hermes Hamarat Poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Published by: hamaratlabs (Yusuf Hamarat) · Contact: yusuf.hamarat@ozu.edu.tr

Hermes Hamarat Poster ("the app") is a self-hosted tool by hamaratlabs that uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's own TikTok account(s). It is not a public service and has no user registration. This page explains what data the app uses and how.

In short

  • No public sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Data is never sold or shared. It is sent only to the TikTok API.

Information the app collects

  • TikTok authorization tokens: the OAuth access token and refresh token that TikTok issues after the account owner grants permission.
  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Videos and captions: these are content created by the owner himself. The app does not collect content from other people.

How the information is used

  • To upload the owner's videos to the connected TikTok account (scope video.upload). Until the app passes TikTok's review, videos arrive as drafts in the TikTok inbox and the account owner publishes them himself in the TikTok app.
  • The app does not read other users' data and does not access third-party accounts.

Storage and sharing

  • Tokens and profile information are stored only in the self-hosted tool on the owner's own computer.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are used.
  • Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is sent only to TikTok, through the TikTok API, to perform the upload.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • When the connection is removed, the stored tokens are deleted.
  • To request deletion of any data, email yusuf.hamarat@ozu.edu.tr.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if the video.publish permission is added), this page will be updated and the date renewed.
