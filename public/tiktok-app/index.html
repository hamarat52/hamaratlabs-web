Hermes Hamarat Poster

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Published by: hamaratlabs (Yusuf Hamarat) · Contact: yusuf.hamarat@ozu.edu.tr

Hermes Hamarat Poster is a self-hosted publishing tool run by hamaratlabs. It sends short videos that the owner has created to the owner's own TikTok account(s).

What it is (and is not)

  • A private tool used only by its owner, Yusuf Hamarat (hamaratlabs), for his own TikTok account(s).
  • It is not a public service: there is no user sign-up and no user registration.
  • It does not access or post to any third party's TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in on TikTok's official login screen (TikTok Login Kit) and grants permission.
  2. The tool uses only the permissions that were granted, to upload the owner's video to TikTok.
  3. Until the app passes TikTok's review, uploaded videos arrive as drafts in the TikTok inbox. The account owner reviews them and publishes them himself in the TikTok app.

Permissions (scopes) requested

  • user.info.basic: the public display name and avatar, used only to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • video.upload: to upload a video to TikTok as a draft.

If video.publish is added in the future, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated.
