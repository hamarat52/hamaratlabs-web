tiTimer: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Publisher: hamaratlabs (Yusuf Hamarat) · Contact: yusuf.hamarat@ozu.edu.tr

tiTimer is a mobile app for tracking focus and break time. This page explains what the app does with data.

In short

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not collect any personal data from you, and does not send it to a server, sell it or share it.

Information that stays on your device

  • The topics you create, timer sessions, unplanned breaks, time you add manually and the notes you write for it, and your settings (default work and break durations) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this information. You can also delete everything permanently at any time with Settings → "Tüm verileri sıfırla" (Reset all data).

Internet and permissions

  • tiTimer does not connect to the internet. The Android version does not request the internet permission; fonts are bundled with the app.
  • It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification permissions.

Device backups

If your phone's own backup feature is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud Backup on iPhone), the operating system may include app data in the backup stored in your own account. This is a function of the operating system, not of the app; you manage it in your device settings.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date renewed.
