tiTimer: Support

tiTimer is a calm focus and break timer for seeing where your time goes. Write to us with questions, bug reports or suggestions.

Contact

Email: yusuf.hamarat@ozu.edu.tr

When reporting a bug, please include your phone model, operating system version and the screen where the problem happens.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. tiTimer does not ask for an account; your records stay only on your device.

Does it need the internet? No. The app works without connecting to the internet.

What is an unplanned break? If you tap "Molayı başlat" (Start break) while the timer is running, the time you spend away is not subtracted from your work; it is saved separately as an unplanned break. Reports show it on its own and do not add it to your focus time.

I forgot to start the timer. Use "Manuel süre ekle" (Add time manually) on the Reports screen to add it later by choosing the topic, duration and day.

How do I delete my data? Settings → "Tüm verileri sıfırla" (Reset all data). This permanently deletes the timer and all records. Uninstalling the app also deletes the data.
