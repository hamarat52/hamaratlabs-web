Hamarat Labs: Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Published by: hamaratlabs (Yusuf Hamarat) · Contact: yusuf.hamarat@ozu.edu.tr

Hamarat Labs: Publisher ("the app") is a self-hosted tool by hamaratlabs (Yusuf Hamarat) that publishes the owner's own posts to the owner's own LinkedIn member profile and to the Hamarat Labs LinkedIn Page he administers. It is not a public service and has no user registration. This page explains what data the app uses and how.

In short

  • No public sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Data is never sold or shared. It is sent only to the LinkedIn API.

Information the app collects

  • LinkedIn authorization tokens: the OAuth access token and refresh token that LinkedIn issues after the account owner grants permission.
  • Basic profile information (OpenID Connect scopes openid, profile, email): member ID, name, profile photo and email address, used only to show which LinkedIn account is connected.
  • Posts, images and videos: these are content created by the owner himself. The app does not collect content from other people.

How the information is used

  • To create posts on the owner's own LinkedIn member profile (scope w_member_social).
  • To create posts on the Hamarat Labs LinkedIn Page, which the owner administers (scope w_organization_social, only if LinkedIn grants this permission to the app).
  • The app does not read other members' data, connections, messages or feeds, and does not access third-party accounts.

Storage and sharing

  • Tokens and profile information are stored only in the self-hosted tool on the owner's own computer.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are used.
  • Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is sent only to LinkedIn, through the LinkedIn API, to create the posts.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time on linkedin.com: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services.
  • To request deletion of any data, email yusuf.hamarat@ozu.edu.tr. The stored tokens are then deleted from the tool.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a new LinkedIn permission is added), this page will be updated and the date renewed.
