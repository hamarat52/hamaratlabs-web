KADER: play in the browser
https://hamaratlabs.com/okul-projeleri/kader/

The game (kader.dsk) runs unchanged inside an Amstrad CPC 6128 emulator.

EMULATOR
  tiny8bit CPC emulator by Andre Weissflog
  https://github.com/floooh/chips       (emulator chips, zlib licence)
  https://github.com/floooh/chips-test  (emulator program, MIT licence)
  https://github.com/floooh/sokol       (platform layer, zlib licence)
  Built with Emscripten 7.0.0 (MIT / NCSA licence) from:
    chips      7ef8776f637a6424ac615da64de6b968b06de791 (unmodified)
    chips-test 9e9c330e0ba5385049d0adac725b3f73eed2f26d (ALTERED, see below)
    sokol      f5e8833 (unmodified)
  Files: cpc.js, cpc.wasm.
  ALTERED VERSION: in chips-test examples/emus/cpc.c and fibs.ts the KC Compact
  ROMs and the 'kccompact' machine type were removed, and the status bar under
  the screen is hidden (only shown with a 'statusbar' URL argument, which this
  page does not pass). Nothing else
  was changed. The full change is in chips-test.patch next to this file.
  This is not the original program by Andre Weissflog.

AMSTRAD ROMS
  The emulator contains the Amstrad CPC 464 and CPC 6128 system ROMs and the
  AMSDOS disc ROM, with their copyright messages unchanged. Parts of the ROMs
  are (c) Locomotive Software.
  Amstrad have kindly given their permission for the redistribution of their
  copyrighted material but retain that copyright.
  Source of the permission (Cliff Lawson, Amstrad, 31 Aug 1999, posted to
  comp.sys.sinclair and comp.sys.amstrad.8bit):
  https://worldofspectrum.net/app/themes/wosc-classic/static/legacy/amstrad-roms.txt
