kader

ka·der · Turkish

In Turkish, kader means it is already known where a life will arrive.

Every decision, every reaction, everything that will happen.

And still, the choice is yours.

Fate holds the strings.
Not all of them.

One finger is cut. It holds no string.

That missing string is your own will.

Free will or fate? Every culture asks. KADER lets you play the question.

You play fate, not God.
